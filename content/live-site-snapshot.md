## About Me

I'm a stay-at-home mom who believes the quality of the food we eat really matters. That's why I use organic ingredients in all my bakes — no exceptions. If I wouldn't feed it to my family, I won't feed it to yours.

What started as baking for my own family grew into sharing with neighbors, friends, and now you. Every loaf, cookie, and cinnamon roll comes from my home kitchen, made in small batches with simple, honest ingredients.

## The Menu

### Organic Sourdough Bread

**Classic Sourdough Loaf**
The original — a crusty, chewy loaf with an open, airy crumb. Perfect for toast, sandwiches, or just warm with butter.

**Garlic & Rosemary Loaf**
Fresh garlic and rosemary folded into our classic sourdough. Savory, aromatic, and impossible to stop eating.

**Everything Seasoned Loaf**
Topped with our everything blend — sesame, poppy seed, garlic, onion, and salt. Crunchy, savory, and packed with flavor.

### Cookies

**Chocolate Chip Cookies**
Crisp edges, soft centers, and plenty of chocolate. The classic, done right.

**Snickerdoodles**
Soft and pillowy, rolled in cinnamon sugar. A little tangy, a lot of comfort.

**Ginger Molasses Cookies**
Deep, dark molasses with warm ginger spice. Chewy, rich, and perfectly spiced.

### Cinnamon Rolls

Available as a single roll or a 4-pack
